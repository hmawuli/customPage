## Getting Started

Welcome to CMS Application! This guide will help you create and manage your personalized web pages without any coding knowledge.

### What You Can Do

- Create unlimited customizable pages
- Choose from beautiful pre-designed themes
- Upload your own images and logos
- Track visitor statistics
- Publish/unpublish pages instantly
- Restore previous versions of your content

---

## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Registration

1. Visit the registration page: `https://yourdomain.com/register`
2. Fill in your details:
   - **Full Name:** Your name or business name
   - **Email Address:** Your email (used for login)
   - **Password:** Choose a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
   - **Confirm Password:** Re-enter your password

3. Click **"Create Account"**

### Step 2: First Login

1. After registration, you'll be redirected to your dashboard
2. For future logins, visit: `https://yourdomain.com/login`
3. Enter your email and password
4. Click **"Sign in"**

---

## Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard is your control center. Here's what you'll see:

### Statistics Cards

- **Total Pages:** Number of pages you've created
- **Total Views:** Combined views across all your pages
- **Unique Visitors:** Number of different people who viewed your pages

### Views Chart

- Visual representation of page views over the last 30 days
- Hover over points to see exact numbers

### Your Pages List

- Quick access to all your pages
- See which pages are published (green badge) or drafts (yellow badge)
- Action buttons: **View** (for published pages) and **Edit**

## Creating Pages

### Step 1: Start Creating

1. From your dashboard, click **"Create New Page"**
2. Or go to **Pages** → **"Create New Page"**

### Step 2: Basic Information

1. **Page Title:** Give your page a descriptive name
   - Example: "John's Portfolio" or "My Business Page"
   - This will be used in the URL (e.g., `/page/johns-portfolio`)

2. **Choose a Theme** (Optional):
   - Select from 6 pre-designed color themes
   - Or choose "No theme" to customize later
   - You can always change this later

3. Click **"Create Page"**

### Step 3: You're Ready to Edit!

After creation, you'll be taken directly to the page editor where you can customize your content.

---

## Editing Pages

The page editor is divided into two main areas:

### Left Panel: Editor Controls

**Four Tabs:**

#### 1. Content Tab

This is where you edit all text on your page:

**Hero Section:**
- **Hero Title:** Main headline (appears large at the top)
- **Hero Subtitle:** Supporting text below the title

**About Section:**
- **About Heading:** Section title
- **About Text:** Your description or story (can be multiple paragraphs)

**Services Section:**
- **Services Heading:** Section title
- **Service Cards:** Each service has:
  - **Title:** Service name
  - **Description:** What you offer

**Contact Section:**
- **Contact Heading:** Section title
- **Email:** Your contact email
- **Phone:** Your contact number

**Tips for Content:**
- Keep titles short and impactful
- Use clear, friendly language
- Break long text into paragraphs
- Focus on benefits to your visitors

#### 2. Theme Tab

**Predefined Themes:**
- Click any theme to apply it instantly
- Each theme includes 6 coordinated colors
- Themes available:
  - Classic Black & White
  - Ocean Blue
  - Forest Green
  - Sunset Orange
  - Royal Purple
  - Modern Brown

**Custom Colors:**
- Override theme colors with your own
- Click color boxes to open color picker
- Customize:
  - Primary (main brand color)
  - Secondary (complementary color)
  - Accent (highlights)
  - Background (page background)
  - Text (body text)
  - Heading (title text)

**Color Tips:**
- Use high contrast for readability
- Stick to 2-3 main colors
- Test on different devices
- Consider your brand identity

#### 3. Images Tab

Upload three types of images:

**Logo:**
- Your business or personal logo
- Appears in the header
- Recommended size: 300x300px
- Formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP (max 5MB)

**Hero Background:**
- Large banner image
- Appears behind hero text
- Recommended size: 1920x1080px
- Use high-quality images

**Profile Image:**
- Your photo or avatar
- Appears in About section
- Recommended size: 500x500px
- Works best with square images

**How to Upload:**
1. Click **"Choose File"** for the image type
2. Select image from your computer
3. Wait for "Image uploaded successfully!" message
4. Image appears in preview immediately

**Image Tips:**
- Use compressed/optimized images for faster loading
- Choose relevant, high-quality photos
- Ensure images are well-lit and clear
- Test how images look on mobile devices

#### 4. Versions Tab

Track and restore previous versions of your page:

- Each save creates a new version
- See who made changes and when
- Click **"Restore"** to revert to a previous version
- Useful if you want to undo recent changes

### Right Panel: Live Preview

- See your changes in real-time
- No need to save to preview
- Shows exactly how visitors will see your page
- Scroll to see all sections

### Saving Your Work

**Two Save Options:**

1. **Save Draft:**
   - Saves changes without making page public
   - Page remains private
   - Good for work-in-progress

2. **Publish:**
   - Saves and makes page publicly accessible
   - Visitors can view your page
   - Page appears at: `/page/your-page-slug`

## Applying Themes

### Using Predefined Themes

1. Go to **Theme** tab in editor
2. Click on any theme preview
3. Colors apply instantly
4. Save your page to keep changes

### Creating Custom Colors

1. Go to **Theme** tab
2. Scroll to **Custom Colors** section
3. Click each color box to open picker
4. Choose your color
5. Changes apply immediately in preview
6. Save to keep changes

### Reverting to Theme

If you've applied custom colors and want to go back to a theme:

1. Click a predefined theme again
2. This will remove custom colors
3. Save changes

## Uploading Images

### Image Requirements

- **Formats:** JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP
- **Maximum Size:** 5MB per image
- **Recommended Dimensions:**
  - Logo: 300x300px
  - Hero: 1920x1080px

### Upload Process

1. Open **Images** tab in page editor
2. Click **"Choose File"** button
3. Select image from your computer
4. Wait for upload (shows progress)
5. Success message appears
6. Image displays in preview

### Managing Images

- To replace an image, simply upload a new one
- Previous image is automatically removed
- Images are optimized automatically for web

## Publishing Pages

### Making Your Page Public

**From Editor:**
1. Click **"Publish"** button (top right)
2. Page is now live and publicly accessible
3. Your page URL: `/page/your-slug`

**From Pages List:**
1. Find your page
2. Page shows "Published" badge

### Unpublishing Pages

To make a published page private again:

1. Open page in editor
2. Click publish button (it will say "Update & Publish")
3. Or use the toggle in pages list

### Sharing Your Page

Once published, share your page by:
1. Copy the URL: `https://yourdomain.com/page/your-slug`
2. Share on social media
3. Add to email signatures
4. Include in marketing materials

**Your Page URL Format:**
- Example: `https://yourdomain.com/page/john-doe`
- The slug is created from your page title
- SEO-friendly and easy to remember

## Viewing Analytics

### Accessing Analytics

1. Click **"Analytics"** in main navigation
2. Or go to dashboard and click on a page

### Understanding Your Analytics

#### Overview Stats (Top Cards)

**Total Views:**
- Number of times your page was viewed
- Includes repeat visits
- Resets based on selected time period

**Unique Visitors:**
- Number of different people
- Identified by IP address
- More accurate measure of reach

**Returning Visitors:**
- People who visited multiple times
- Shows engagement level
- Higher is better!

#### Time Period Selector

Choose from:
- **Today:** Views from today only
- **Last 7 Days:** Past week
- **Last 30 Days:** Past month (default)
- **Last Year:** Annual stats

#### Views Chart

- Visual timeline of page views
- Hover over points for exact numbers
- Identify traffic patterns
- Spot peak days/times

#### Top Referrers

- Shows where visitors came from
- Examples:
  - Google (search traffic)
  - Facebook (social media)
  - Direct (typed URL)
  - Other websites linking to you

### Exporting Analytics

1. Select your page
2. Choose time period
3. Click **"Export CSV"** button
4. Download opens in Excel/Sheets
5. Contains detailed view data

**CSV Includes:**
- Date and time of each view
- IP addresses
- Browser information
- Referrer (where they came from)
- New vs returning visitor

### Using Analytics Insights

**High Views, Low Conversions:**
- Improve your call-to-action
- Make contact info more prominent
- Clarify your offering

**High Bounce Rate:**
- Improve page loading speed
- Make content more engaging
- Ensure mobile-friendliness

**Traffic Sources:**
- Double down on what works
- If social media works, post more
- If search works, improve SEO

## Managing Your Pages

### Pages List

Access from **Pages** menu to see all your pages:

**Each Page Shows:**
- Title
- Status (Published/Draft)
- Theme name
- Creation date
- Public URL (if published)

**Actions Available:**
- **View:** Opens public page in new tab (published only)
- **Edit:** Opens page editor
- **Delete:** Permanently removes page (asks for confirmation)

### Duplicating Pages

To create a copy of an existing page:

1. Open page in editor
2. Click **"Duplicate"** (if available)
3. Enter new title
4. New draft page is created
5. Edit as needed

### Deleting Pages

To remove a page:

1. Go to **Pages** list
2. Click **"Delete"** button
3. Confirm deletion
4. Page is moved to trash (soft delete)
5. Can be restored by admin if needed

**Warning:** Deleted pages lose all analytics data. Export analytics before deleting if you need the data.

## Tips & Best Practices

### Content Writing

**Keep It Simple:**
- Use short sentences
- Avoid jargon
- Write like you talk
- Focus on benefits

**Structure Content:**
- Use headings effectively
- Break text into small paragraphs
- Use bullet points (in text)
- Highlight key information

**Call to Action:**
- Tell visitors what to do next
- "Contact me today"
- "Learn more"
- "Get started"

### Design Tips

**Choose Colors Wisely:**
- Use brand colors if you have them
- Ensure good contrast
- Test readability
- Be consistent across pages

**Images:**
- Use high-quality photos
- Compress before uploading
- Choose relevant images
- Professional photos work best

**Mobile-First:**
- Test on phone/tablet
- Keep text readable
- Ensure buttons are tapable
- Check image sizing

### SEO Basics

**Page Title:**
- Include keywords
- Keep under 60 characters
- Make it descriptive
- Unique for each page

**Content:**
- Use natural keywords
- Write for humans first
- Include location if local business
- Update regularly

**URLs:**
- Keep slugs short
- Use keywords
- Avoid special characters
- Make them readable

### Performance

**Fast Loading:**
- Optimize images before upload
- Don't use huge images
- Keep content concise
- Avoid too many images

**Regular Updates:**
- Update content monthly
- Refresh images seasonally
- Check for broken links
- Update contact information

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Can't Login**
- Check email spelling
- Verify password (case-sensitive)
- Use "Forgot Password" if needed
- Clear browser cache
- Try different browser

**Image Won't Upload**
- Check file size (max 5MB)
- Verify file format (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP)
- Try smaller image
- Check internet connection
- Try different browser

**Changes Not Saving**
- Check internet connection
- Look for error messages
- Try saving again
- Refresh page and re-edit
- Contact support if persistent

**Page Not Showing**
- Ensure page is published
- Check if using correct URL
- Clear browser cache
- Try incognito/private mode
- Verify no server issues

**Theme Colors Not Applying**
- Click theme again
- Clear custom colors first
- Save after applying
- Refresh preview
- Try different theme first

**Analytics Not Updating**
- Wait up to 5 minutes
- Refresh analytics page
- Check time period selected
- Verify page is published
- Test by visiting page yourself

### Getting Help

**Check Logs:**
- Note any error messages
- Take screenshots
- Note exact steps taken

**Contact Support:**
- Email: support@yourdomain.com
- Include:
  - Your email/username
  - Page title affected
  - What you were trying to do
  - Error message (if any)
  - Screenshots

**Self-Help Resources:**
- Check README.md for technical details
- Review this user guide
- Search for similar issues
- Check system status page

## Glossary

**Analytics:** Statistics about who visits your pages and how they interact with them.

**Dashboard:** Your main control panel showing overview of all pages and stats.

**Draft:** A page that is saved but not publicly visible.

**Published:** A page that is live and publicly accessible via its URL.

**Referrer:** The website or source that brought a visitor to your page.

**Slug:** The URL-friendly version of your page title (e.g., "my-awesome-page").

**Theme:** A pre-designed set of colors that can be applied to your page.

**Unique Visitor:** A person visiting your page, counted once regardless of how many times they visit.

**Version:** A saved snapshot of your page at a specific point in time.
